# ☕ Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes Bright Coffee Shop sales data using **Databricks, SQL, Microsoft Excel, Miro, Canva, and Microsoft PowerPoint**. The objective is to identify sales trends, top-performing products, peak sales periods, and provide actionable business recommendations that support informed decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales performance
- Identify top-selling products
- Determine peak sales periods
- Evaluate product categories
- Develop data-driven business recommendations

## 📊 Key Findings

- Coffee generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- The busiest sales period was between **09:00 and 11:59**.
- Coffee was the best-selling product category by quantity sold.
- Earl Grey Regular was one of the highest-selling individual products.
- Evening trading hours recorded the lowest sales.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand coffee products.
- Increase staffing during peak morning trading hours.
- Introduce promotions during off-peak periods to increase customer traffic.
- Bundle coffee with bakery products to increase average transaction value.
- Monitor low-performing products and implement targeted marketing strategies.
