# ☕ Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes Bright Coffee Shop sales data using Databricks, SQL, and Microsoft Excel. The objective is to identify sales trends, top-performing products, peak sales periods, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve business performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales performance
- Identify best-selling products
- Determine peak sales periods
- Evaluate product categories
- Provide business recommendations

## 📈 Key Findings

- Coffee generated the highest revenue.
- Peak sales occurred between 09:00 and 11:59.
- Earl Grey Regular was one of the best-selling products.
- Coffee was the most popular product category.
- Evening sales were the lowest.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Increase inventory for high-demand products.
- Schedule more staff during peak hours.
- Introduce promotions during slow periods.
- Bundle coffee with bakery products.
